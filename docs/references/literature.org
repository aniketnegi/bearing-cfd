* Gangrade, Phalle, and Mantha

Ajay Kumar Gangrade, Vikas M. Phalle, and S. S. Mantha,
“Performance Analysis of a Conical Hydrodynamic Journal Bearing,” /Iranian
Journal of Science and Technology, Transactions of Mechanical Engineering/,
volume 43, pages 559--573; published online in 2018.

- DOI: [[https://doi.org/10.1007/s40997-018-0217-2][10.1007/s40997-018-0217-2]]
- Local author copy:
  [[file:../../../../docs/papers_sorted/conical-journal-bearing/cfd/Performance Analysis of a Conical Hydrodynamic Journal Bearing.pdf][Performance Analysis of a Conical Hydrodynamic Journal Bearing.pdf]]

** Relevance to this project

The paper analyzes the lubricant clearance using a modified Reynolds equation
in a conical/spherical-coordinate formulation and solves the resulting
thin-film problem by finite elements on a developed conical surface.  It
motivates the periodic circumferential/axial master coordinates and the
thin-film context.  It does not validate this project's three-dimensional feed,
native-BREP assurance, or Tri3-to-Prism6 construction; those are local design
decisions implemented in
[[file:../../meshing/layered_prism_central_feed.py::def build_master_mesh][the local master-surface construction]] and
[[file:../../meshing/layered_prism_central_feed.py::def build_prism_mesh][explicit Prism6 construction]].
