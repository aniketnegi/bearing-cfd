#+TITLE: Paper-Input Reynolds--JFO Results
#+OPTIONS: toc:2 num:nil

* Status

The native OpenFOAM continuation converged and passed its pressure-floor and
flow-balance gates at 0, 20, 496.563, 1000, and 2000 rpm on the 256 by 80 grid.
These are accepted numerical results with physical and grid validation still
open.

* Mesh

=blockMesh= creates 20,480 two-dimensional finite-volume cells with cyclic
circumferential boundaries, atmospheric axial boundaries, and an =empty=
front/back pair.

The minimum pressure is the imposed JFO rupture floor, not a negative-pressure
excursion.  The large rupture area and low fill remain unvalidated physical
predictions.

* Independent implementation comparison

Against the independent Python solver on the same grid at 2000 rpm, maximum
absolute pressure differs by -0.0256 percent, pressure RMS by 1.11 kPa, fill
RMS by =2.03e-4=, feed flow by 0.00018 percent, force components by at most
0.022 percent, and torque by 0.00022 percent.

The paper figure gives approximate =Pmax/Ps= values of 35.6 for FEA and 32.0
for Fluent.  This is a comparison of one scalar, not validation of the full
pressure field, rupture front, load, flow, or torque.
