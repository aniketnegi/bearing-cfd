* Scope

This repository constructs and validates the lubricant-fluid domain of an
eccentric conical journal bearing with a radial central feed.  It contains:

- exact build123d/OCCT B-rep construction of the film and feed passage;
- native-BREP assurance inside Gmsh/OpenCASCADE;
- a full-360 no-port structured Hex8 regression mesh;
- a film-only structured Hex8 mesh with a surface pressure-inlet patch;
- a body-fitted circular O-grid Hex8 mesh with a native Fluent handoff and
  full-3D Orthogonal Quality gate;
- a retained conformal central-feed layered Prism6 full-passage mesh;
- clean MSH 4.1, MSH 2.2, and CGNS interchange from canonical NumPy arrays;
- optional OpenFOAM and Fluent import audits in the mesh-generation pipeline;
- a separately documented OpenFOAM 14 study on the validated S100
  surface-inlet Hex8 mesh.

Solver meshes describe the lubricant control volume, not the metal assembly.
Their annular centre is empty because the journal occupies that space
physically; separately exported journal/bushing STEP solids are visual context,
not additional fluid zones.

The canonical CAD and mesh-generation commands stop before assigning CFD
physics.  The downstream OpenFOAM study is intentionally separate and is
recorded in [[file:docs/handoff/00-index.org][the CFD handoff]].  It includes
accepted commissioning and low-speed liquid checkpoints, rejected diagnostics,
and a guarded saturation-threshold stop.  It does not yet contain an accepted
two-phase cavitation result.

The STEP failure is isolated to neutral-format translation; native BREP is the
accepted CAD handoff to the open-source mesh pipeline.

The earlier Fluent S100 solve log is rejected divergence evidence, not a
canonical import-pass result.  The current body-fitted circular O-grid handoff
has 1,252,800 Hex8 cells in *12 physical thickness layers*, passes the
full-volume standard Fluent-equivalent Orthogonal Quality gate at
=0.9226601271 >= 0.9=, and is still
=STATICALLY_VALIDATED_NOT_IMPORTED= until Fluent Mesh Check and Report Quality
are run.  At 20 rpm the OpenFOAM branch remains
single-liquid and passes its acceptance gates; the 20.2-rpm update crossed the
configured =pSat= threshold and was stopped before being treated as cavitation.
The later native Reynolds/JFO midsurface application reaches 2000 rpm.  Its
active-set kernel is consistent with graph-read peaks and fronts from a
published 60-cell slider comparison, but the conical model's feed footprint is
grid-dependent and its 64.455% severe rupture region has not passed full-model
verification.  It is preserved for validation, not reported as trusted bearing
performance.
